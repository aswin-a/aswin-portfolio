JUDGMENT RULES

Four rules the whole stack runs on

  1. R1

    AI prepares, I decide

    Research, drafts and options come from AI. The call, and the accountability, stay with me.

  2. R2

    Verify before it ships

    Numbers, quotes and claims are checked against their source before anyone else sees them.

  3. R3

    Context over prompts

    Good output starts from good context: strategy, personas, metrics and past decisions, kept current.

  4. R4

    Prune what doesn’t earn its place

    If a tool or agent doesn’t save time or sharpen thinking, it comes out of the stack.
