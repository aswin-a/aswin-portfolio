Position

Product Manager (Procurement)

About JiBe

JiBe is a cloud-based fully integrated ERP system for the shipping industry. Our goal is to allow shipping companies to improve productivity, efficiency, and safety levels, while reducing costs. JiBe ERP enables increased automation and streamlining processes, creating pre-defined workflows, and reducing the usage of email and paper.

Job Responsibilities

Define and execute the product roadmap for Procurement & Vendor Management modules.
Gather customer requirements and translate them into user stories and functional specifications.
Work closely with Engineering, QA, Sales, and Implementation teams throughout the product lifecycle.
Conduct market research and competitor analysis to identify product improvements.
Drive new feature development, testing, release, and adoption.
Act as the subject matter expert for procurement processes and best practices.
Use customer feedback and data insights to enhance user experience and product value.

Experience with B2B SaaS products, ERP systems, or procurement solutions.

Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills.
Maritime, shipping, supply chain, or ERP product experience preferred.
Experience in Procurement, Supply Chain, or Maritime ERP products.
Familiarity with SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics, JiBe, or similar ERP platforms.
Experience creating wireframes, mockups, and UI requirements.
